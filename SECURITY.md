# Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@kots.club**. Don't open a public issue or pull request, and
don't test against mainnet in ways that could affect other users' funds.

Helpful to include: the program and address concerned, what an attacker could do, and
the steps or a proof of concept that shows it.

We'll acknowledge your report as soon as we can and keep you informed until it's fixed.
Program upgrades require the project's Squads multisig, so fixes are deployed through a
multisig vote.
